## Things I've built

### [Merge Film Festival](https://mergefestival.ch) · [`mff`](https://github.com/tommyzaffa/mff)

The festival's site in four languages and the system its first edition ran on (1-4 October 2026):

- passes and tickets paid by card or TWINT (Stripe), confirmation emails (Resend), digital badges with Google Wallet
- QR scanning at the screening room doors and a box office page for the staff
- the audience vote, open from the start of each screening
- live captions for the talks, translated between Italian and English in real time (Soniox)

Static front end on GitHub Pages, Supabase back end (Postgres, RLS, Edge Functions). 180 accreditations and 867 audience votes went through it.

### [PoPaCi Studio](https://studio.popaci.com) · private repo

The internal platform of a film production company: screenplay editor with PDF export, project boards, a shared calendar, chat with video calls, a whiteboard, and the festival module where the Merge selection committee and juries scored the films. Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase.

### [Stecco the Stickman](https://tommyzaffa.github.io/stecco-the-stickman/) · [`stecco-the-stickman`](https://github.com/tommyzaffa/stecco-the-stickman)

A first-person game where everything is drawn in pencil. Three.js and TypeScript, with every texture, sound and piece of music generated in code. Plays on desktop and on phones.

### [Echo Maze](https://echomaze.quest) · [`echo_maze`](https://github.com/tommyzaffa/echo_maze)

A single-player metroidvania that runs in the browser. No download, no launcher.

### Lugano in anteprima · [`lugano-in-anteprima`](https://github.com/tommyzaffa/lugano-in-anteprima)

A prototype for trying out a day in Lugano on an illustrated map before actually going out. OpenStreetMap data, official timetables, swisstopo relief.
